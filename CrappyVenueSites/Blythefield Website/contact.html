3G Pitch at Blythefield

Our state-of-the-art 3G football pitch is available for hire by local teams, schools, and community groups.

📅

Availability

The pitch is available throughout the week. Weekday evenings and weekends are popular, so we recommend booking in advance.

💰

Affordable Rates

As a charity, we keep our prices accessible for the community. Contact us for current rates and any available discounts for local groups.

📝

How to Book

Fill out the contact form above or call us on 028 9033 3527 with your preferred dates and times. We'll confirm availability and send you booking details.
