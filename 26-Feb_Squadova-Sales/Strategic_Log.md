## 2026-01-12 - Initial Sales Strategy Session

### Context
First strategic session with Claude as cofounder advisor. Reviewed full business plan and 53 facilities in pipeline.

### Key Discussion: Facility Prioritisation

**Question:** Which facilities to target first, and should they get free-for-life or free-for-one-year?

**Decision:** Use tiered approach based on "building a fire" principle.

**Tier 1 - Immediate Targets (Free for Life)**
- Blythefield - Meeting already scheduled. Charity, small, perfect first pilot.
- Hanwood Centre - Small community centre, already contacted. Follow up aggressively.
- Ballyhornan - Rural, low tech sophistication, would appreciate free software.

**Tier 2 - Secondary Pilots (Free for 1 Year)**
- Score FC - Warm intro via Jackie. 5 pitches, uses PitchBooking. Real migration opportunity.
- St. Malachy's - Uses Word/Excel. Summer applications give time to perfect product.

**Tier 3 - Wait for Proof**
- Universities (UU, QUB) - Explicitly need to see it working elsewhere first.
- Grosvenor - Joe MIA. Don't chase.
- Shaftesbury - Follows Grosvenor's lead.

### Pricing Framework Established

| Offer | When to Use |
|-------|-------------|
| Free for Life | First 3-5 facilities. Charities. Detailed feedback + testimonial required. |
| Free for 1 Year | Facilities 6-15. Larger ones with real revenue. |
| Paid | Only after 5+ testimonials and proven metrics. |

### Key Insight
The social platform (player matchmaking) is the moat. PitchBooking is just booking. Squadova promises to actually increase revenue by bringing players to empty slots.

1. Crush the Blythefield meeting - prepare demo, listen more than talk
2. Follow up with Hanwood Centre - need second small facility in pipeline
3. Email Jackie about Score FC warm intro
4. Build case study template - what metrics to track at Blythefield for showing universities later
