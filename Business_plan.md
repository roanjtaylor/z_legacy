# Squadova Business Plan

## Personal Experience of the Problem

In early 2025, I tried organising a game with friends and hit a wall—busy schedules, scattered groups, impossible logistics. When I travelled, I wanted to play but couldn't find available players or games in new cities.

**The purpose of sport for the average person nowadays is to have fun—they are games.** The character development and life lessons come naturally once the individual is hooked from inherently enjoying playing (as they will actually show up consistently if they like it).

Yet, most clubs emphasise hard training for competitive matches, which deters the average person. Life responsibilities require energy—not everyone can commit to official clubs.

**This business exists to empower anyone to organise their ideal football game when it suits them, regardless of external factors (e.g. location, available friends, schedule).**

Sport should be easy and fun—not hard and painful.
