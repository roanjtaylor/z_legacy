About Us

Discover what makes Grosvenor Recreation Centre the heart of our community

Our Story

Grosvenor Recreation Centre is a vibrant and inclusive community-managed facility located at 162 Grosvenor Road, Belfast. Just a five-minute walk from Belfast City Centre, we serve as a hub for sport, health, and community development in the local area.

Managed by the Roden Street Community Development Group, our centre is dedicated to providing accessible, high-quality facilities and programmes that make a real difference in people's lives. From grassroots sports to family support, we bring the community together.

We believe that everyone deserves access to quality recreation and wellness services, regardless of background or ability. That's what drives us every single day.

What We Offer

Comprehensive services for the whole community

⚽

Community Sports

Our 3G pitch and indoor sports hall host football leagues, training sessions, and casual games for all ages and skill levels throughout the week.

💪

Health & Wellbeing

We run a range of health and wellbeing programmes designed to improve physical and mental health across our community.

👪

Family Support

Our family support services provide guidance, resources, and a welcoming space for families to get the help they need.

🌱

Environmental Projects

We're committed to environmental sustainability through community-led projects that benefit our local area and beyond.

🎓

Capacity Development

Training and development programmes that equip community members with skills and confidence to achieve their goals.

🏊

Gym & Fitness

Our gym is equipped with modern facilities for all fitness levels, with competitive pricing and a friendly, supportive atmosphere.
