About GVRT

Serving the Greater Village community since 1999

Our Story

Greater Village Regeneration Trust (GVRT) is a community organisation with charitable status, established in 1999 to re-develop the Greater Village area and address physical decline in the area.

We were set up in response to a decline in the social and economic condition of the South-West Belfast area, with particular reference to housing. Today, we work across a range of thematic issues including health and wellbeing, training and employability, physical regeneration, housing, and community safety.

As a community-led organisation based at 337 Donegall Road, we deliver a wide range of initiatives to support local people to improve their lives through personal development, housing and environmental improvement schemes, employability courses, and health and wellbeing activities.

Our Vision

We're working to produce a confident community that lives side-by-side with other communities, has improved relationships with statutory agencies and government departments, and contributes individually and collectively to the overall renaissance of Belfast City.

Community First

The intended beneficiaries of all our work are the residents of south-west Belfast, namely the wards of Blackstaff and Shaftesbury.

Inclusivity

We serve adults, children, youth, older people, unemployed residents, ex-offenders, and interface communities.

Empowerment

We help local people develop skills, find employment, and improve their quality of life through education and personal development.

Collaboration

We work alongside statutory agencies, local organisations, and volunteers to maximise our positive impact in the community.

Our Programmes

We deliver a comprehensive range of services to support our community.

Fit 4 Life Centre

Our flagship health and wellbeing programme delivers a range of activities supporting four key areas:

  • Mental Health - Support and activities for emotional wellbeing
  • Physical Health - Fitness sessions and sports programmes
  • Social Health - Community connection and group activities
  • Environmental Health - Improving our local surroundings
💼

Training & Employability

We provide comprehensive support to help local people into employment:

  • Job search and application support
  • CV writing and interview preparation
  • Personal and vocational courses to upskill local people
  • Relief of unemployment through practical assistance
🏠

Housing Rights & Support

GVRT runs a daily housing rights and support service for people in the local area, providing:

  • Housing advice and advocacy
  • Support for those in housing need
  • Environmental and physical improvement work
  • Liaison with housing authorities
🎓

Adonis & Athena Youth Programmes

Our dedicated youth programmes support young people in their transition to adulthood through:

  • Community education
  • Personal development guidance
  • Life skills and confidence building
  • Positive activities and mentoring

3G Football Pitch at Blythefield

Our state-of-the-art 3G pitch provides quality sports facilities for the community:

  • Available for hire by local teams and groups
  • Suitable for training and matches
  • Affordable rates for community organisations
  • Well-maintained, all-weather surface
🚌

Community Minibus Hire

We operate a minibus rental service available to community organisations, schools, youth groups, sports teams, and statutory agencies at affordable rates.
